# Commercial model

Apache-2.0 supports a paid Moxnox Omni service. The recommended open-core
boundary is:

## Open core

- Provider-neutral event contracts.
- Webhook verification and normalization.
- Deterministic automation engine.
- Basic human-review workflow.
- Community channel adapters.

## Operated service

- Hosting, uptime, backups and incident response.
- Provider onboarding and Meta App Review assistance.
- Managed upgrades, observability and delivery reconciliation.
- Customer-specific flows and content governance.

## Separately licensed candidates

- Multi-tenant billing and plan enforcement.
- Advanced analytics and agent evaluation.
- Organization SSO, audit exports and regulated-retention controls.
- Proprietary connectors, managed AI routing and premium support.

Keep proprietary modules in separate repositories or clearly separated
packages with their own license headers. Do not accept external contributions
that need future relicensing without an appropriate contributor agreement.

Releases already distributed under Apache-2.0 cannot be made non-Apache for
existing recipients. Brand names, domains, hosted data, credentials, prompts
and service operations are not granted by the source-code license.

This is a product strategy record, not legal advice. Have Brazilian counsel
review the final corporate ownership, contributor terms, privacy documents and
customer contract before paid launch.
